What is Buna?

Buna is the Amharic word for coffee. In Ethiopia, coffee is more than a drink. It is a way of welcoming guests, bringing families together, and sharing time with friends and neighbors.

Ethiopia is known as the birthplace of coffee. Many people believe coffee was first discovered in the Kaffa area, where coffee plants grew naturally.

Why People Love Ethiopian Coffee

  • It has a rich and natural flavor.
  • It comes from different Ethiopian coffee-growing regions.
  • It is connected to Ethiopian culture and hospitality.
  • It is often served during the traditional coffee ceremony.
  • It can have fruity, floral, sweet, or strong flavors.
