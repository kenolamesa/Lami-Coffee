Traditional Coffee Ceremony

The Ethiopian coffee ceremony is an important part of daily life. It is not only about drinking coffee, but also about respect, conversation, and community.

  1. The green coffee beans are washed.
  2. The beans are roasted until they become dark and aromatic.
  3. The roasted beans are ground by hand.
  4. The coffee is boiled in a traditional pot called a jebena.
  5. The first round of coffee, called abol, is served.
  6. The second round, called tona, is served.
  7. The third round, called baraka, is served.

Important Coffee Terms

Buna
The Amharic word for coffee.
Jebena
A traditional Ethiopian clay coffee pot used during the coffee ceremony.
Abol
The first round of coffee served during the ceremony.
